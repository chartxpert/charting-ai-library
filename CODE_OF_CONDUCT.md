# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender
identity and expression, experience level, education, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

- Be respectful and constructive. Arabic and English are both welcome.
- No harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.
- No publishing others' private information.
- Accept and give feedback gracefully.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to **support@chartxpert.io**. All reports are
reviewed confidentially. Maintainers may remove content or ban participants
who violate this code.
